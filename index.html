Asset Recovery Assistance

This website exists to confirm the identity of the sender if you received communication referencing Unclaimz regarding potential unclaimed financial assets.

Why you may have been contacted
Our records sometimes indicate that funds or other property may be held in the name of an individual, family member, or estate. If you were contacted by us, we are offering assistance in evaluating and pursuing a potential claim.

Who We Are

Unclaimz is a private asset recovery service operated by Keystone Discovery Group LLC.

What This Page Is For

This page is intended as a public-facing verification page for individuals who were contacted directly.

How the process works

  1. We review records that may indicate unclaimed financial assets.
  2. We assess whether an individual or estate may have a potential basis to pursue a claim.
  3. If appropriate, assistance is offered under a written agreement.
Services, when offered, are provided only after a written agreement is executed. Nothing on this website constitutes a guarantee of recovery or a promise that any particular claim exists or will be approved.
